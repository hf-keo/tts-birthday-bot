## Birthdays and server administration
Register only information you are entitled to provide. Administrators must obtain a member’s permission before entering that member’s birthday. Do not submit birth years, ages, or other sensitive information. Registered day/month information is visible to members of the same server through birthday commands and may appear in the configured announcement channel. Administrators choose channels, mentions, and whether announcements are enabled.

Use /birthday register to update your entry and /birthday remove to remove it in each server. Removal does not erase past Discord messages or delivery-history records; see the [Privacy Policy](https://github.com/hf-keo/tts-birthday-bot/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).
